About Joseph Farah

30 Years of Experience
in Beauty & Personal Care

Based in Zahle, Lebanon, Joseph Farah brings over 30 years of professional experience in beauty, personal care, and specialized treatments. Throughout his career, he has built his expertise around precision, quality, and personalized service, helping both men and women look and feel their best.

Joseph offers a complete range of professional services, including Makeup Artistry, Skincare, Tattoo Art, Permanent Makeup, Nail Art, Medical Pedicure, Laser Treatments, Solarium, and Hair Styling.

With decades of experience and a passion for his craft, Joseph combines professional techniques with individual attention to ensure every client receives a comfortable, personalized, and high-quality experience.
